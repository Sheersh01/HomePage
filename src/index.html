ABOUT US

TANTRAFIESTA

TantraFiesta is the National Level Annual Technical Fest of the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Nagpur. TantraFiesta'25 is coming back this year with more than 30+ interesting events under the theme "Dark Matter Eclipse: Exploring the Unexplored." This theme symbolizes venturing into the unknown realms of technology, innovation, and imagination — pushing boundaries to discover what lies beyond the visible horizon.
