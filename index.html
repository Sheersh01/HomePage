TANTRAFIESTA

TantraFiesta is the National Level Annual Technical Fest of the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Nagpur. TantraFiesta'24 is coming back this year with more than 30+ interesting events under the theme "Digital Big Bang: Uncharted Realms Await." This theme captures the exponential growth in the evolution of technology in areas concerning breakthroughs in Artificial Intelligence — especially in Large Language Model development — and the enormous possibilities of power unleashed through GPUs.
